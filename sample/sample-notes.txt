Machine Learning Notes

Overfitting occurs when a model learns the training data too well, including noise, and performs poorly on unseen data. Common remedies are regularization (L1/L2), dropout, early stopping, more training data, and cross-validation.

Underfitting happens when a model is too simple to capture the underlying pattern. Increase model capacity or train longer to fix it.

The bias-variance tradeoff: high bias means underfitting, high variance means overfitting. Good models balance both.

Gradient descent updates parameters in the direction of the negative gradient of the loss. Learning rate controls step size; too large diverges, too small converges slowly.
